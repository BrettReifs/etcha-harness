# Context layers

Etcha supplies reusable practice, not a component library or product template.
The broader personal agent system and C4 map remain future work.

The small [Etcha skill](../.github/skills/etcha-design/SKILL.md) is the source
of truth for layer boundaries. Its linked adapters preserve product and design
contracts. The task brief applies the four reduction laws before generation.
Do not repeat those rules in product documentation.

An adopter keeps its users, evidence, tokens, components, copy, and deliberate
exceptions in its own repository. Unknown facts remain unknown. A neutral test
fixture is not a recommended product identity.

Impeccable supplies discovery, design documentation, critique, live iteration,
and detectors. Etcha adds context boundaries, a bounded brief, common evidence
reporting, verification adapters, and safe adoption. It does not fork the skill.
